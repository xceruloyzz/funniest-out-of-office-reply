Entry for the [Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8) bounty on make.fun — one line, at most 160 characters, the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a colleague will read all week.

## The entry

> I'm away until Monday. My email is being handled by my cat, who has already deleted three of yours and slept on the rest.

121 characters. `main.py` writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) — directly to the final file, no temp files, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
